# Compensation adjustment policy

## Annual review

Salary adjustments are decided once a year in April. The budget is 8 percent of
the department payroll, and a manager cannot exceed it without approval from
the HR head.

## Out of cycle increases

An out of cycle increase is allowed only for a promotion or a counter offer.
It needs written approval from both the HR head and the finance head.

## Confidentiality

Individual compensation figures must not be shared outside the HR team, even
with the employee's own manager's manager.
