# Travel and expense policy, version 3

## Meal reimbursement

The meal reimbursement limit is 4000 rupees per day in metro cities and 2500
rupees per day elsewhere. Receipts are no longer required below 2000 rupees.

## Travel class

Train travel in AC 2 tier is now reimbursed in full. Air travel still needs
approval from a manager before booking.

## Claim deadline

Claims must be submitted within 45 days of returning. Claims older than that
need a written exception from the finance head.
