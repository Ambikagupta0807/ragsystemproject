# Remote work policy


## Eligibility


Full-time employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely
up to two days per week. Remote work is subject to manager approval and the
requirements of the employee's role.


## Request and approval


Employees must submit a remote-work request to their reporting manager at least
three business days before the requested date. Managers may approve or decline
the request based on team coverage, operational requirements, and performance
needs.


## Working hours


Employees working remotely must remain available during their normal working
hours and attend scheduled meetings. They must inform their manager in advance
if they need to change their working location or working hours.


## Equipment and security


Employees must use their company-issued laptop and approved company tools while
working remotely. Company information must not be accessed on shared devices or
public Wi-Fi networks without an approved VPN connection.


## Exceptions


Employees who need a temporary remote-work arrangement for medical, family, or
other exceptional reasons should contact HR and their reporting manager.
