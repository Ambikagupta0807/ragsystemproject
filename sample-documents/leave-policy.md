# Leave policy

## How many leaves

Employees get 24 paid leaves in a calendar year, plus 6 casual leaves that do
not carry forward.

## Applying

Leave must be applied at least 3 days in advance, except sick leave, which can
be applied the same day with a message to the manager.

## Carry forward

Unused paid leaves up to 10 days carry forward to the next year. Anything above
that lapses on 31 December. Loss of pay applies once both paid and casual
leaves are finished.
