# Shipping and delivery

## Dispatch times

Orders placed before 2 pm are packed the same day. Orders after that move to the
next working day.

## Delivery times

Delivery to metro cities takes 2 working days. Other cities take 4 to 5 working
days. Remote pin codes can take up to 8 days. A tracking link is sent by email
and SMS when the courier picks up the parcel.

## Delivery charges

Free delivery applies on orders above 999 rupees. Below that a flat fee of 49
rupees is added at checkout.
