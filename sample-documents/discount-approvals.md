# Discount approval limits

## Approval ladder

A support agent may approve a goodwill discount up to 500 rupees without asking
anyone. A team lead may approve up to 5000 rupees. Anything above that needs
the finance head.

## Bulk orders

Orders above 50 units get a dedicated account manager and a negotiated price
that must be recorded in the deal sheet before invoicing.

## What is never allowed

Discounts are never applied after an invoice is generated. A credit note is
issued instead, so the books stay auditable.
