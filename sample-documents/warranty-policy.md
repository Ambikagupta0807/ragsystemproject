# Warranty

## What is covered

All electronics carry a one year warranty against manufacturing defects,
starting from the delivery date.

## What is not covered

The warranty does not cover physical damage, liquid damage, or damage caused by
using a charger that did not come with the product.

## Making a claim

Raise a ticket with the order id and a photo of the issue. A pickup is arranged
within 3 working days in serviceable areas. Replacement is offered when repair
is not possible.
