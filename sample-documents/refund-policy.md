# Refund policy

## Refund window

A refund can be requested within 7 days of delivery. The item must be unused
and in its original packaging. Opened software, gift cards and items marked
final sale cannot be refunded.

## How long a refund takes

Once the returned item reaches our warehouse, the refund is processed in 5 to 7
working days. The money goes back to the original payment method. For cash on
delivery orders, the refund is sent to the bank account shared by the customer.

## Cancelling instead

If an order has not shipped yet, cancelling is faster than refunding, because
the amount is released the same day.
