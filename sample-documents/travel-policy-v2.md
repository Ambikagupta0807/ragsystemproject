# Travel and expense policy, version 2

## Meal reimbursement

The meal reimbursement limit is 3000 rupees per day in metro cities and 2000
rupees per day elsewhere. Receipts are required above 1500 rupees.

## Travel class

Train travel in AC 3 tier is reimbursed in full. Air travel needs approval from
a manager before booking.

## Claim deadline

Claims must be submitted within 30 days of returning.
